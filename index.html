ABOUT WOODCREST

Owner-direct flooring services.

Woodcrest Floors installs all types of wood flooring, including traditional hardwood, parquet, and patterned layouts. Repairs and custom staining are completed with state-of-the-art equipment and care at each stage.

Owner John Gilligan works directly with clients. More than 20 years in the flooring business have shaped a service built on reliable communication, integrity, and professional workmanship. Woodcrest is licensed and insured, and all work is guaranteed to the customer's satisfaction.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

For other questions, call (516) 695-4761.

What subfloors are compatible?

Woodcrest works with many types of existing surfaces, including plywood and wood subfloors, existing hardwood, concrete, ceramic, vinyl, and marble. We’ll assess your specific conditions and recommend the right preparation.

Do you provide custom staining?

Yes. Custom staining is available for refinishing and repair projects. We can discuss color options and how they’ll look with your wood and lighting.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on the room size, selected flooring, layout, and preparation needed. Contact us with a few details about your project for a more useful estimate.

Is there a warranty?

Ask us about coverage for your particular project. We’ll explain the applicable workmanship and product terms when we prepare your estimate.
